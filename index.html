Established under Act No. XLV of 1975

The Area Study Centre for Africa, North and South America was formally created under the Area Study Centres Act No. XLV of 1975 passed by the Parliament of the Government of Pakistan.

Functioning as an autonomous statutory institution, the Centre is funded by the Federal Ministry of Education through the Higher Education Commission (HEC), while maintaining full academic integration with the Faculty of Social Sciences at Quaid-i-Azam University, Islamabad.

Autonomous Status

Governed by its statutory Board of Governors ensuring executive independence and academic autonomy in research design.

Federal HEC Grants

Directly funded by the Federal Ministry of Education via HEC for cutting-edge archives, research publications, and faculty development.

QAU Academic Wing

Degrees are awarded under the academic seal of Quaid-i-Azam University's Faculty of Social Sciences.

Tri-Continental Scope

Pakistan's sole specialized research institute dedicated to Africa, North America, and South America.

Fostering Global Regional Expertise & Policy Leadership

Welcome to the Area Study Centre for Africa, North and South America at Quaid-i-Azam University. Established under the statutory framework of the 1975 Area Study Centres Act No. XLV, our Centre serves as Pakistan's premier seat of academic research on foreign policy, political economy, and security across three continents.

As we inaugurate our flagship 4-Year BS Degree in Area Studies starting this current semester, we expand our academic tradition beyond MPhil and PhD research. This undergraduate program offers young scholars early specialization in American foreign policy, African trade corridors, and Latin American diplomatic alliances.

I invite prospective students, international fellows, and policy analysts to engage with our faculty and explore our peer-reviewed publications.

Undergraduate Degree Curriculum

BS Area Studies (4-Year Program)

Quaid-i-Azam University • Starting Current Semester

1. Program Overview

The Bachelor of Science in Area Studies provides comprehensive multidisciplinary training spanning foreign policy, strategic relations, history, economy, and comparative politics across North America, South America, and Africa.

2. Semester Curriculum Breakdown

Semesters 1 - 2 (Foundations)
  • Intro to Area Studies & Research
  • Global History of 20th Century
  • Fundamentals of Political Science & IR
  • Academic Writing & Foreign Language I
Semesters 3 - 4 (Regional Core)
  • North American Political Systems
  • African Diplomatic & Trade History
  • Latin American Geopolitics
  • Foreign Policy Analysis
Semesters 5 - 6 (Advanced Tracks)
  • US Grand Strategy & Great Power Politics
  • Sub-Saharan Political Economy
  • MERCOSUR & Inter-American Blocs
  • Quantitative Research Methods
Semesters 7 - 8 (Capstone)
  • Regional Security & Defense Seminars
  • Specialization Elective (US / Africa / Latin America)
  • Mandatory Senior Thesis / Capstone Project

3. Eligibility & Merit Formula

Candidates holding FA/FSc/A-Levels or equivalent qualification with minimum 50% aggregate marks are eligible to apply. Merit is calculated as per Quaid-i-Azam University regulations.
