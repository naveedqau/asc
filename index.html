Message from the Director

Leading higher research and academic exchange in Area Studies

Director, ASC

Area Study Centre, QAU

Quaid-i-Azam University, Islamabad

"Welcome to the Area Study Centre for Africa, North and South America at Quaid-i-Azam University. Since its establishment under the 1975 Act No. XLV, our institution has remained at the forefront of post-graduate research, policy analysis, and academic inquiries regarding the Western Hemisphere and the African continent."

Our mission is to foster a deep, objective, and multidisciplinary understanding of the socio-political, economic, and strategic developments shaping North and South America as well as Africa. Through our M.Phil. and Ph.D. programs, international partnerships (including the Ball State University collaboration), and our flagship publication—the Pakistan Journal of American Studies (PJAS)—we equip scholars and researchers to contribute meaningfully to global diplomatic discourse and national policy development.

We invite students, visiting scholars, and researchers to explore our extensive research library, participate in our seminars, and collaborate with us in advancing area studies in Pakistan.

Institutional Mandate

Promoting advanced research and specialized academic understanding

1975 Act No. XLV

Established as a statutory autonomous body under Act No. XLV of 1975 by the Parliament of Pakistan to promote study and research on foreign regions.

HEC & Ministry Funded

Directly funded by the Federal Ministry of Education through the Higher Education Commission (HEC), guaranteeing high academic standards and resources.

QAU Academic Integration

Academically part of Quaid-i-Azam University and affiliated with the Faculty of Social Sciences, awarding M.Phil. and Ph.D. degrees to qualified candidates.

Academic Programs

Multidisciplinary postgraduate degrees designed to develop world-class foreign policy and regional analysts.

Master of Philosophy

M.Phil. in American Studies

A rigorous 2-year program offering specialized courses on U.S. domestic politics, foreign policy, Latin American affairs, African geopolitical studies, and international security.

  • Duration: 2 Years (4 Semesters)
  • Eligibility: 16 Years Education (MA/BS) in Social Sciences
  • Coursework + Research Thesis
