Institutional Overview

Pioneering Regional & Hemispheric Studies in Pakistan

The Area Study Centre for Africa, North & South America was established under the statutory provisions of the 1975 Area Study Centres Act No. XLV by the Government of Pakistan. Operating as an autonomous institution, the Centre is directly funded by the Ministry of Education through the Higher Education Commission (HEC).

Academically tied to Pakistan’s flagship university, Quaid-i-Azam University (QAU) in Islamabad, the Centre operates under its prestigious Faculty of Social Sciences. It serves as a vital bridge for understanding the strategic, diplomatic, economic, and socio-cultural dimensions of the Western Hemisphere and the African continent.

Our Mission

To conduct high-caliber research, offer advanced degree programs, and advise national policymaking bodies on transatlantic and inter-american affairs.

Governance

Governed by an independent Board of Governors comprising representatives from QAU, HEC, Ministry of Foreign Affairs, and eminent scholars.

Director's Statement

"Understanding foreign regions is essential for dynamic diplomacy and economic engagement."

Research Divisions

Tri-Continental Academic Focus

Our interdisciplinary research is organized across three primary regional desks providing specialized insights.

Division I

North America Studies

Covers US Foreign Policy, US-Pakistan Bilateral Relations, Canadian Political Economy, Transatlantic Security, and Congress Legislative Analysis.

  • US Presidential Elections & Policy
  • Defence & Strategic Alliances
  • US Trade & Global Governance
Division II

South America & Caribbean

Focuses on MERCOSUR economic dynamics, Brazil/Argentina emerging power roles, Latin American regional integration, and trade linkages with South Asia.

  • Latin American Geopolitics
  • Economic Blocs & Trade Agreements
  • Caribbean Maritime Diplomacy
Division III

African Union & Regional Desk

Researching the African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA), Pakistan's "Look Africa" Policy, Horn of Africa maritime security, and AU conflict resolution.

  • Look Africa Policy Framework
  • African Union & Regional Security
  • Trade & Diplomatic Expansion

Higher Degree Opportunities

Postgraduate Degree Programs

Affiliated with the Faculty of Social Sciences, Quaid-i-Azam University.

Degree Program • 2 Years

M.Phil. in Area Studies

A rigorous coursework and research thesis program designed for students aiming for expertise in North/South American or African political and social systems.

Eligibility: BS/MA in IR, Pol Sci, History
Entry Test: QAU Subject Test / GAT
Seats: 20 Students per intake
Doctoral Program • 3-5 Years

Ph.D. in Area Studies

Advanced doctoral research focusing on original empirical and theoretical contributions to American policy, diplomacy, or African regional integration.

Eligibility: M.Phil in relevant discipline
Evaluation: Foreign Review + Defense
Publication: HEC Category Article Required
Short Diploma • 6 Months

Diplomatic & Regional Certificate

Tailored for foreign service mid-career officers, journalists, and researchers needing specialized briefings on transatlantic trade & diplomacy.

Format: Evening / Hybrid Classes
Target Audience: Diplomats, Analysts, Media
